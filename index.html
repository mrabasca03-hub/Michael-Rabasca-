PROFILE

In my current role as Director of Investment Operations and Accounting at UJA-Federation of New York, I lead investment operations and accounting processes for a $3B endowment and pension portfolio, reporting directly to the Chief Investment Officer (CIO). As the CIO’s operational right hand, I own audit, reporting, cash, trade execution, manager due diligence and investor relations, enabling the investment team to focus on the continuing success of the portfolio.

Prior to my role at UJA, I helped build a New York family office from its startup phase. As Senior Fund Accountant, I designed the internal controls, reporting processes, and treasury and trade workflows across more than fifty entities and every major asset class. Earlier in my career, I advised private equity clients at the consulting firm CFGI to help clients improve operational efficiency and find solutions to their everyday problems. I started my career in audit at Deloitte focusing in real estate.

As a creative thinker and operations professional, I like building rather than maintaining; finding the work that has no obvious owner, putting structure and documentation around it so it no longer depends on one person, and using automation and AI to give the team capacity back. I manage up to senior leadership and down to a team I hold accountable for accuracy and deadlines.
