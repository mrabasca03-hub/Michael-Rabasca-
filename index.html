PROFILE

My work sits where accounting meets operations. At a New York family office I built the internal control and reporting framework from the ground up during the firm's startup phase — NAV review, capital calls and distributions, trustee fee calculations, treasury forecasting and IFRS audited financial statements across private equity, venture, hedge funds, cryptocurrency and public equities.

Before that I advised private equity clients at CFGI and led audit engagements for real estate and alternative asset managers, including SEC registrants. I taught myself Power BI to replace manual reporting, and I have deployed data feeds and AI extraction tools to take recurring work off people's desks.

I like teams that are building something — where the process does not exist yet and someone has to design it correctly the first time.
